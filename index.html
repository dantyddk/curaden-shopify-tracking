Metric definitions & calculation logic
Definitions below reflect the formulas used in this dashboard. All monetary calculations follow the selected currency toggle.
Order scope & launch splitPost-launch KPIs include completed and paid orders only; refunded orders are excluded. UAE benchmark ends 25 Aug 2026 and post-launch starts 26 Aug. Switzerland benchmark includes 3 Sep 2026 and post-launch starts 4 Sep.
GMV_incTaxMerchandise value including tax and shipping.Σ(Line-item quantity × Line-item price) + Shipping
GMV_excTaxGMV after removing tax.GMV_incTax − Taxes
Net_incTaxCustomer payment remaining after payment-gateway cost.Actual Customer Paid − Paygatefee_inclTax
Orders & purchased usersOrders count eligible order IDs. Purchased users count unique customer emails per day.Orders = COUNT(Order ID) · PU = COUNTUNIQUE(Email)
Average order valueAverage GMV generated by each eligible order.AOV = GMV_incTax ÷ Orders
%Promo burnDiscount cost relative to GMV excluding tax.Discount Amount ÷ GMV_excTax
Promo orderAn order is promotional when it has a discount amount, a discount code, or a mapped promo type other than “No Discount”.%Promo Order = Promo Orders ÷ Total Orders
%Average promo discountWeighted discount rate across promotional orders.Σ Discount ÷ Σ(GMV_incTax + Discount)
%Paygate feePayment-gateway cost as a share of the value collected after discount.Σ Paygatefee_inclTax ÷ Σ(GMV_incTax − Discount)
Promotion type distributionOrder share, total discount cost, average cost per order and weighted discount rate are calculated within promotional orders only.%Promo orders = Promo-type Orders ÷ All Promo Orders
Currency conversionSource post-launch values are consolidated in EUR. Local-currency display reverses the country’s EUR conversion rate.EUR = Local Amount × Currency Rate to EUR
